# Changelog

The module got its own repository at version 1.3.0; the entries before it are summaries.

## 1.4.0

- Molding: any particle can be shaped into another outline by hand (drag its points, draw it, or start from a shape),
  in the studio, the manager, or on the map with the new Mold tool. Rings, glyphs, glows and stars follow it.
- Effect walls have layers: lines beside the path and stars across a loop. Four seals come built in, and a rune circle
  can become a seal on the same spot, along a path you stretch over a whole room.
- A wall's path can be made all corners or all curves in one click; an effect's motion can be shaped by hand.
- Built-in looks can be changed for the world and reset; Save as mine makes a copy.
- Looks being edited on the map reuse one texture per token.
- Windows: the studio, the manager and the effect walls manager can be made much smaller (they rearrange as they
  narrow), open where and as big as you left them, step aside (collapsed to their title bar) while you place
  particles, emitters or casts or draw a wall's path, and come back when you are done (Escape or another tool). They
  no longer redraw under an open dropdown, and the manager's list scrolls instead of squashing its rows.

## 1.3.0

- Who controls particles: each particle has its own owners (only the GM, chosen players or everyone), set in the
  Particle Manager. Effects choose who controls what they cast; by default, whoever controls the caster.
- Particles can be up to 100 squares, and big particles get sharper textures.

## 1.2.0

- Effect walls: fire, chains, lightning, ice, thorns, runes, force, shadow, water or a beam painted along any animator
  path, shown all at once, growing, running round like a train or following a token, optionally blocking movement
  or sight.
- The Particle Manager, and Ctrl+Z for every change.
